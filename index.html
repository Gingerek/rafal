About

I photograph what is easy to overlook

People, places and brief moments in which light creates atmosphere.

I look for natural emotion, calm compositions and images that remain in memory longer than the event itself. My work moves between portrait, street, travel and visual detail, but the common thread is always mood and an honest moment.
